## Frequently Asked Questions
* **"The server kicks me, says something about AntiTCC..."**

The server is using an outdated version of AntiTCC. You can't do anything on your part except ask the serveradmin to get an updated version of AntiTCC from [our AntiTCC-Releases repo](https://github.com/oldunreal/AntiTCC-releases).

* **"I play in [X] language, but I don't have audio files!"**

You may be playing in a pre-Preview 11 version, where this problem has been fixed.

## Compatibility issues with other mods
* **[foxWSfix](https://github.com/alexstrout/foxWSFix-UT2k4):** From Preview 11 onwards, this fix is not needed, as UT2004 has its own Hor+ widescreen implementation.
* **Many other mods that change the GUIController class ([Random Ladder Mod](https://www.moddb.com/mods/random-ladder-mod), [MapMixer (in GUI form)](https://unrealarchive.org/unreal-tournament-2004/mutators/M/mapmixer2361nov2007_582778b9.html)...):** Be aware that these mods were created with a 3369 or previous version of the game's code, hence there may be certain compatibility issues that can't be solved unless those mods' codebases are adapted to work with v3374.
